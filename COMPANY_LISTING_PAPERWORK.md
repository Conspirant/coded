## D. Business Description

KCETCoded is an education-technology platform providing web-based counselling research and decision-support tools for KCET and COMEDK applicants. The platform enables users to explore historical cutoff data, research colleges and courses, use rank-estimation and planning tools, access counselling-support information, and use preparation and community features.

The company provides informational software services through its web application. The platform is independent and is not an official service of KEA, COMEDK, any educational institution, or any government authority.

## H. Service and Product Statement

KCETCoded provides information and software-based decision-support tools. Historical data, estimates, predictions, simulations, community content and AI-assisted responses are informational outputs. Users are responsible for verifying examination, counselling, eligibility, rank, document, fee, seat, college and admission decisions through the relevant official authority and source documents.

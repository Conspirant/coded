## 1. Executive Summary

Every year, between **2,50,000 and 3,20,000 students** in Karnataka appear for the Karnataka Common Entrance Test (KCET) and COMEDK to secure undergraduate seats in engineering, architecture, farm science, and medical courses.

Despite the critical nature of this process, the counseling system remains complex, fragmented, and stressful:
- Official Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA) cutoffs are published across **hundreds of pages of unsearchable, dense PDFs** spanning multiple rounds and complex reservation quotas.
- Middle-class and rural students often lack access to professional counseling, leading to costly errors during Choice Entry, suboptimal college selection, or exploitation by predatory admission agents demanding exorbitant fees.
- Quality private institutions like **Sai Vidya Institute of Technology (SVIT)** spend large sums each admission season on traditional billboards and newspaper advertisements that deliver single-day visibility with minimal measurable student engagement.

**KCETCoded** bridges this gap. It is a high-speed, data-verified counseling intelligence platform that makes all historical cutoffs, round movements, rank simulations, and college profiles instantly searchable, completely free for students.

By formally sponsoring and hosting KCETCoded, **Sai Vidya Institute of Technology (SVIT)** gains:
1. Direct, high-intent brand visibility to **over 2,50,000 engineering aspirants and their families** during their most critical decision-making window.
2. A transparent, high-converting admissions pipeline focused on candidates scoring within SVIT’s target KCET rank brackets (ranks 45,000 to 95,000).
3. Measurable institutional prestige and accreditation points under NAAC, NBA, and MHRD Innovation Council (IIC) frameworks for incubating an impactful student-led digital public utility.

## 3. The Student-First Mission & Ethical Guidelines

To protect the integrity of the platform and preserve student trust, the following ethical charter governs all features, sponsorships, and user interactions:

1. **The Zero-Paywall Guarantee:**
   All core counseling functions—including the Cutoff Explorer, Rank Predictor, Round Analysis, and Option Entry Simulator—shall remain **completely free and accessible to every student**, regardless of background or financial status.
2. **Deterministic Truth & Data Verification:**
   The platform never fabricates or speculates on cutoff numbers. Every record links to official KEA/COMEDK source documents, citing exact page numbers and PDF hashes.
3. **Transparent Institutional Relationship:**
   SVIT’s sponsorship is presented transparently as an **Incubation and Infrastructure Partner** supporting a free public utility. The platform does not alter objective rank data or artificially inflate admission chances.
4. **Data Privacy & Candidate Protection:**
   The platform complies strictly with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act of India. Student phone numbers, email addresses, and rank details are never sold, rented, or distributed to third-party telemarketing databases.
